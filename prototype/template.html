How to use this prototype. Keepers: sign in with any keeper code (K0566, K1483, K0303…) and password carrowmore, then choose an open role to see how your record compares to the requirements. Employers: sign in with any email and password array, then select a candidate to see how their experience compares to open roles.

How this was made. From station logbooks (cleaned by the Authority) and training records only. Appraisals and station manager notes are never read. An AI read every log sentence and marked which job requirements it shows; it was checked against a person's grading on ten keepers before this page existed (EVALUATION_PLAN.md).

What the labels mean. Qualified and/or Certified: the certificate is held and in date. Nearly Qualified (Partial Cert): some modules held, or the certificate has lapsed. Logged experience, needs validation or cert: the logs show this work at least twice, but nothing formally confirms it yet; it needs confirming with the keeper, or a certificate that isn't on record. Insufficient Documented Qualifications: the logs don't show this work clearly enough yet, or there is no certificate or medical in the training records. Neither is the same as not being able to do it.

There is no match score. Keepers can correct or add to anything, and nothing reaches an employer without the keeper seeing it first.

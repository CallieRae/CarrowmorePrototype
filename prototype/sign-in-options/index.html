Three ways in to the sign-in

Each option opens on the station at night and brings you to the same sign-in. They differ in how the light arrives and how long you wait for it. Any key or tap skips the intro, and anyone who prefers reduced motion goes straight to the card.

Fixed across all three: the card is Mist at 75% opacity, all type is Black at 100%, and the sign-in works as it does in the prototype.

A · About 5 seconds

First Light

The lamp is out. It strikes, the light travels outward along the beams and lifts the whole scene, then the card rises. Built on the illustration as supplied.

Open First Light
B · About 4 seconds

Beacon

The scene redrawn as vector art, so the beams start fully retracted and extend from the lantern while circuit traces draw along them. The beams keep a slow breath afterwards.

Open Beacon
C · About 5.5 seconds

Approach

Arriving by boat: the view opens close on the dark lantern, the lamp strikes, and the view draws back to the whole station as the light comes up.

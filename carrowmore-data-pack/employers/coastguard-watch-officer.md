# Watch Officer — Northern Isles Coastguard Service

**Grade:** Watch Officer (Operational)
**Location:** Cnoc Maritime Rescue Coordination Centre
**Posts:** 5
**Pattern:** rotating watch cycle including nights, weekends and public holidays

---

### About the role

Watch Officers maintain the continuous distress and safety watch for the Northern Isles
search and rescue region, receive and assess incoming alerts, and coordinate the response
of declared and assisting units until an incident is closed.

The work is long periods of vigilance interrupted by short periods of extremely high
demand. You will spend most of a twelve-hour night watch monitoring traffic that requires
no action from you at all, and then be expected to take a poorly worded distress call, form
an accurate picture from incomplete information, and start the right things happening
within ninety seconds.

### Essential criteria

Candidates must be able to demonstrate, with examples:

1. **Sustained concentration.** The ability to maintain attention and judgement across a
   full twelve-hour night watch, repeatedly, over a rotating cycle. This is the single most
   common reason candidates are unsuccessful at assessment.
2. **Written accuracy under pressure.** Incident logs are legal documents. They are read
   at inquests. We will assess your written English by timed exercise and we do not make
   allowances for it afterwards.
3. **Radiotelephone discipline.** Experience of maritime VHF procedure, DSC alerting, and
   the conventions of distress, urgency and safety traffic.
4. **Situational judgement.** The capacity to decide, on partial information, what is
   probably happening at sea and what to do about it.
5. **Composure.** Incidents end badly with some regularity. You will be expected to
   continue the watch afterwards.

### Desirable criteria

- Experience of on-scene coordination or of acting as a reporting point in a live incident
- Meteorological observing and the ability to read a developing situation from the weather
- Experience of keeping a formal operational record over a long period
- Any background in a service requiring continuous watchkeeping

### What we are not looking for

Physical fitness beyond ordinary health, and any form of offshore or height qualification,
are irrelevant to this post. Please do not include them. We mention this because we
receive a great many applications from people whose strengths are entirely physical and
who find the watch itself intolerable within a month.

### Terms

Public service terms, incremental scale, watch allowance. Full training provided over an
initial nine-month probation. Appointment subject to security vetting and a hearing test.

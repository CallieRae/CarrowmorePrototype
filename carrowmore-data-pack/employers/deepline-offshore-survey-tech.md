# OFFSHORE SURVEY TECHNICIAN

**DEEPLINE OFFSHORE LTD**
Rotational · 4 weeks on / 4 weeks off · Worldwide mobilisation from Cnoc

---

**READ THIS FIRST.** This is a hard job on a working vessel. Twelve-hour shifts, seven days
a week, for four weeks, in whatever the sea is doing. Shared cabins. No alcohol. If any of
that is a problem, stop reading — we would rather not waste your time or ours.

**THE ROLE**

Survey technician on geophysical and construction-support spreads. You are responsible for
the online and offline availability of the survey sensor suite and for getting it back when
it goes down, which it will, at 0300, in a swell.

Responsibilities:

- Mobilisation and demobilisation of survey spreads, including deck layout, cable runs and
  terminations
- Keeping towed and hull-mounted sensors online, and fixing them when they are not
- Fault-finding electrical and electronic systems to component level with what is in the
  container
- Maintaining positioning and telemetry links to the vessel and to remote nodes
- Winch and A-frame operations for deployment and recovery
- Condition of the spread: connectors, terminations, cable dressing, corrosion control
- Helideck duties when rostered
- Handover notes that the opposite shift can actually use

**REQUIREMENTS — NO EXCEPTIONS**

- Valid offshore survival and safety certification (all modules, in date)
- Offshore medical
- Valid passport, willingness to travel at 48 hours' notice
- Demonstrable electrical/electronic fault-finding ability. We will test this at interview
  with a fault on a bench.

**WHAT GETS YOU HIRED**

We hire for one thing above all: whether you can fix something you have never seen before,
at night, with the wrong tools, while somebody senior is asking you how long. Everything
else is trainable.

People who have come to us from lighthouses, from fishing, from the merchant fleet and from
the armed services have all done well. People who have only worked where there was always
somebody more senior to call have generally not.

**RATE**

Day rate, paid for days worked, plus mobilisation. Ask at interview. We do not publish it
and we do not negotiate after offer.

Applications: recruitment@deeplineoffshore.example — CV only, no covering letter, we do not
read them.

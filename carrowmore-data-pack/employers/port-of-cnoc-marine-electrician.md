# Marine Electrician

## Port of Cnoc Harbour Trust

The Trust is looking for a marine electrician to join the engineering department. There
are four of us and we look after everything from the lock gates to the crane on No.2 quay
to the lights in the fish market, so the job is varied and no two weeks are the same.

### The work

Day to day you would be dealing with:

- The quayside distribution boards and shore power connections for visiting vessels
- The lock gate machinery, its motors, limit switches and control panels
- The travelling crane on No.2 and the two fixed derricks
- Standby generation for the lock and the vessel traffic tower
- Navigation lights on the pier heads and the leading lights up the channel
- Whatever has stopped working, which is most of it, most of the time

We have an 11kV intake from the network and a small ring feeding the north quay. You would
not be doing the switching yourself unless you already hold an authorisation, but you would
be working alongside the person who does, and we would put you through it in time if you
wanted it.

### Who we are after

Somebody who can be handed a problem and left alone with it. We are a small department and
there is no supervisor standing over you. If you need to be told what to do each morning
this will not suit you.

You will need to be able to get about the port in all weathers, work from a boat
occasionally, and go into the lock chambers and cable tunnels when they are drained down.
You will need to be reasonably handy with the mechanical side too because we do not have
the luxury of separate trades.

Formal qualifications are welcome but we care more about what you have actually done. The
Trust has taken on people from the fishing fleet, from the Authority and from the yard, and
the ones who worked out were the ones who had fixed things for a living.

### Terms

Permanent, 39 hours, Monday to Friday with a shared standby rota (one week in four).
Overtime is available and is genuinely voluntary. Pension, sick pay, and a berth for a
small boat if you have one.

Apply in writing to the Harbourmaster's office. We do not use an online system.

# Hydrographic Technician

**Meridian Survey (Northern) — a small firm, eleven of us, based at Rathmoyle**

Hello. We survey the seabed. Mostly that means charting surveys for the harbour trusts,
pre-construction work for the wind farms, and a fair amount of aid-to-navigation
verification work for the Authority, which is how you may have heard of us.

We need one more pair of hands, ideally two.

## What you would do

You would be on our 16m survey launch *Cliodhvar* for most of the season, which is April to
October, working out of Rathmoyle and occasionally further north for a week at a time. In
winter you would be in the office processing, which some people love and some people
loathe.

On the boat:

- Running survey lines to a plan and keeping to them in a seaway
- Deploying, recovering and looking after the multibeam head, the motion sensor and the
  sound velocity probe
- Taking and logging sound velocity casts
- Keeping the survey log properly — times, events, equipment changes, everything
- Checking our results against the published characteristics of the marks we pass
- Handling the boat when the skipper is doing something else
- Making the coffee, fixing the generator, and everything else that comes with a small boat

In the office:

- Cleaning the data, which is most of the job
- Producing charts and reports that a harbourmaster can act on
- Telling us when something does not look right, which matters more than anything else on
  this list

## What we need

Honestly? Someone careful. The whole job is about whether you noticed something.

You need to be able to be on a small boat all day without minding. You need a current small
craft and marine operations certificate, or to be willing to get one within your first
season — we will pay. You need to be able to write a clear sentence.

Formal survey experience is nice and is not essential. We have trained two people from
scratch and both are better than the ones who came to us already trained.

## What we cannot offer

A big-company career structure, or any certainty about the winter. We are honest about
this. Some winters are busy and some are three of us in the office wondering.

Write to Fionntan at the office. Tell us about something you noticed once that other people
had missed.

# High Voltage Authorised Person — Carrowmore Array

**Employer:** Carrowmore Array Asset Operations Ltd
**Posts available:** 6 (4 offshore rotational, 2 onshore substation)
**Reference:** CAAO/HV/AP

---

## 1. Purpose of the role

The postholder is appointed in writing as an Authorised Person for switching operations on
the Carrowmore Array 33kV collector system and the associated onshore substation. The
postholder will carry out switching, apply and remove circuit main earths, and issue and
cancel safety documents to persons working on or near high voltage apparatus.

This is a safety-critical appointment. The requirements below are set by our insurers and
by the network operator's connection agreement. **We have no discretion over them.** Please
do not apply if you do not hold them, and please do not ask us to make an exception,
because we cannot.

## 2. Mandatory requirements

Applicants **must** hold, at the point of application:

1. A current High Voltage Authorised Person certificate (HVAP) issued by the Northern
   Marine Competence Board, comprising all four modules including the practical assessment.
2. A current Offshore Safety and Survival Certificate (OSSC), all modules, within its
   four-year validity.
3. Offshore medical fitness certification.
4. Two years of documented switching experience on a system at 11kV or above.

## 3. On requirement 4

We are aware this is where most otherwise excellent applications fail.

The Competence Board will accept a lapsed internal high voltage authorisation issued by a
recognised operator in place of the two-year experience requirement — **but only where the
original authorisation can be evidenced documentarily.** A recollection, a reference, or a
line in an appraisal is not sufficient on its own for the Board, although it may be enough
for us to support an application on your behalf.

If you believe you held such an authorisation and cannot produce the paperwork, contact us
anyway. We have had some success reconstructing these from operator records and we would
rather spend the effort than lose the candidate. We cannot promise anything.

## 4. Desirable

- Experience of issuing safety documents under a formal permit regime
- Experience supervising contractors on live sites
- Willingness to act as a mentor to trainee Authorised Persons

## 5. Terms

Staff appointment. Offshore posts work 2 on / 3 off. Onshore posts are based at the Cnoc
substation on a 4-on 4-off day/night rotation. Salary on application.

Appointment is subject to a formal Authorised Person interview and site familiarisation of
not less than six weeks before any authorisation is issued.
